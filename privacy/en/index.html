Privacy Policy — AI Inc

Effective date: 7 October 2026

AI Inc ("we") is an Android application by VAVA Games with the package identifier dev.aagames.aiinc.

What data is processed

Analytics is disabled by default. Only after you give separate consent does the game send anonymous gameplay events to GameAnalytics: starting and resuming a game, the starting country, ability actions, reaction stages, terminal states and game outcomes. Technical information needed to analyse errors and application stability may also be sent.

GameAnalytics automatically creates a pseudonymous technical installation identifier for analytics. The game does not send its own user identifier to the service.

We do not transmit the contents of saved games, news text or data entered by the user.

Purposes of processing

The data is used to improve AI Inc's balance, gameplay and technical stability.

Consent and withdrawal

Analytics is enabled only after your separate consent. You can withdraw consent and disable analytics in the application's settings; after this, no new analytics events are sent.

Third-party processor

GameAnalytics is used for analytics. It processes data on our behalf in accordance with its policy: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-faq

Advertising

Yandex Mobile Ads and Yandex Mediation, including Google AdMob, are used to show interstitial and rewarded ads. Advertising services may process your IP address, advertising and technical device identifiers, information about your device and the application, diagnostic data, and information about ad impressions, views and interactions. This data is used to show and measure ads, support their operation and prevent invalid traffic.

When connected through Yandex Mediation, Start.io may process IP addresses, advertising and technical identifiers, device, app and network information, and ad interactions. See the Start.io Privacy Policy for details: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/. Start.io may separately request consent for personalized advertising.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to manage advertising consent. When a consent request is required, the application shows a message with options to consent, decline and manage preferences. You can change your choice through the advertising privacy control in the game's menu. Consent to game analytics is requested separately.

For more information about how advertising services process data: Yandex's privacy policy — https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/; Google's use of data on partner websites and applications — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases that permanently unlock AI models. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment card details. To confirm and restore purchases, the application receives purchase information from Google Play (product identifier, purchase status and purchase token) and stores the list of unlocked models on your device. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
